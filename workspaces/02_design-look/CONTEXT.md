# 02 Design the look

Stage contract: Edward's design process (first written for Sabre, `Desktop\Sabre Constructions\docs\reference\design-brief.md`).
Repeating unit: one building's look, or the product site.

## Inputs
- Working: the client's own material (signage, logo, brochure, photos) in `../../buildings/<slug>/photos/` and `documents/`.
- Reference: `../../docs/design/design-brief.md` (palette, references, crafted-vs-AI rules, imagery), the skills
  `/design-taste-frontend` and `/high-end-visual-design` (no round pill tags — Edward's rule).

## Process
1. **Tokens** from the client's own material, not chosen by us. None? Propose from the references, say so.
2. **Reference sites**: live sites in the client's world, each row = URL · the one thing to steal · which page · notes.
   Screenshot desktop + phone and look at each.
3. **Build** one page (or 2–3 options if the client will pick), same content, different skin.
4. **Screenshot** desktop and phone, look, fix, before anyone sees it.
5. **Pick and apply**: the chosen look becomes the building's brand settings (colours, banner, logo) in Settings.

## Outputs
The page(s) in `../../catalyst/client/`, screenshots, the building's brand settings.

## Check
Edward looks at the screenshots. No look is called finished before he (or the client) has seen it.
