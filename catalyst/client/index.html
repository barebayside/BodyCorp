The problem

Requests get lost

They come by email, phone and in the foyer. Half the details are missing, and nobody's sure if the manager or the committee should act.

Deadlines get missed

Some requests come with a legal clock. Miss it and the decision can be made for you.

There's no record

Months later, nobody can say who asked, who decided, or why.

What it does

Forms in your building's colours

Your name, logo and colours. Each form asks only what that request needs, photos included.

One inbox for the manager and committee

Each request goes to whoever handles it. Everyone sees what's open, what's due and who's on it.

Steps and deadlines from your by-laws

Every request type has a checklist and a countdown. Handlers get a reminder each morning.

A permanent record

Every message, step and decision is kept, with who did it, when, and the reasons.

Notices and minutes

Post notices and share minutes, by-laws and the CMS. Owners-only where it should be.

Only what's theirs

Residents sign in with a code sent to their email. They see their own units and nothing else.

How it works

  1. 1

    We set up your building

    Your units, your colours, and your by-laws turned into forms and steps.

  2. 2

    Residents sign up

    They claim their unit. Your approver checks it against the roll.

  3. 3

    Requests arrive tracked

    Each gets a reference, a checklist and a deadline, right through to a decision.

The steps are guidance, checked by your building's advisors.
